# TejeConMedida

## Product truth

TejeConMedida turns a real knitting or crochet swatch into transparent counts for a specific project. The user already has yarn, needles or a pattern and needs to remove arithmetic uncertainty without hiding rounding or assumptions.

## Primary journeys

1. Normalize any measured swatch to stitches and rows per 10 cm.
2. Convert a desired width and height into compatible stitch and row counts.
3. Adapt a rectangular pattern section to a different gauge.
4. Distribute increases or decreases evenly across an existing row.
5. Keep the most recent result locally for reference while working.

## Constraints

- Calculations run in the browser and require no account.
- Results must distinguish horizontal and vertical gauge.
- The interface must show the physical effect of rounding.
- Advice must not imply that drape, elasticity, shaping or yarn usage can be inferred from arithmetic alone.
- Pages must remain fast, printable, accessible and useful on a phone beside the work.
